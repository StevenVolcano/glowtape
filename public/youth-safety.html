🛡️ Youth Safety at Glow Tape

Community theater is full of kids — and an app that serves them has to be built for their safety first, features second. These aren't settings that can be switched off; they're how Glow Tape is designed.

Children never have accounts

Performers under 13 never sign in, never have a login, and never use Glow Tape directly. A child exists in the system only as a record their parent or guardian manages: a first name and last initial, their role, and links to their guardians. We store no email, no phone number, and no birthdate for any child.

Guardians see everything

Every schedule, every call, every reminder, and every group message a child's participation touches goes to their guardians — all of them. Multiple guardians (both parents in any family arrangement) can attach to the same child with the same code, and each independently receives the full picture. There is no channel to a child that a guardian cannot see.

No private messages, by design

Glow Tape has no direct messages at all — for anyone. All conversation happens in group channels visible to the whole cast or crew, following the same "open and transparent communication" principle used by youth-serving organizations nationwide (no one-on-one digital contact between adults and minors). We will not add private messaging that could involve a minor.

Photos require consent

Every member has a photo-consent flag their guardian or production staff controls. When a production includes anyone without photo consent, the app warns before photos are shared. Photos posted in channels stay inside the production — no public galleries, no public links. Theaters should still collect their signed photo releases; Glow Tape honors the answer.

Teens (13–17)

Teenagers may have their own sign-in. Their accounts carry an age band (never a birthdate), and productions should keep a guardian linked so parents stay in the loop until 18. Quiet hours apply to everyone — no text messages between 9pm and 7am; an early-morning call is announced the evening before instead — and teen accounts additionally skip headshot uploads on community profiles.

Anyone can flag anything

Every message has a ⚑ report button (tap the + under a message). Reports go directly to the Glow Tape operator — deliberately not to the production's own staff — and a human looks at every one.

What we don't collect

No birthdates (we ask age once at sign-up, keep only "adult or teen," and discard the number). No medical, allergy, or health information — ever. No advertising trackers. Nothing about anyone is sold, shared for marketing, or used to train AI.

Theaters' part

Glow Tape is a tool; your theater's own youth-protection policies (supervision, background checks, two-adult rules) remain yours and remain essential. If you see anything in the app that concerns you, email callboard@glowtape.net — a human reads it, promptly.
