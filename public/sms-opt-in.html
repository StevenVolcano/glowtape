How Glow Tape collects SMS opt-in consent

Glow Tape (glowtape.net) is a free scheduling app for community theater volunteers in Grays Harbor County, WA, operated by Zucchini Volcano LLC. Text messaging is strictly opt-in, initiated by the end user inside their own signed-in account. This page documents the exact consent flow and the screens as they appear in the app.

Step 1 — The user enters their own number (point of collection)

Rendered exactly as in the app: Home screen → “Text reminders” card.

Text reminders

Add your cell number and Glow Tape will text you before your rehearsal calls — about 10 hours ahead, and again about 2 hours out. Email reminders happen either way.

Example: (360) 555-0123
Text me a code

By tapping Text me a code you agree to receive a one-time verification code and, once you confirm below, recurring rehearsal-reminder texts from Glow Tape (up to 2 per rehearsal day; frequency varies with your schedule). Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See our privacy policy and terms.

Step 2 — Double opt-in: the number is verified

A 6-digit one-time code is texted to the number; the user must type it back into the app. No reminder texts are ever sent to a number that hasn't completed this verification.

Step 3 — Recorded consent, revocable anytime

Shown after verification; the user can un-check it at any time.

  • One-time sign-in / verification codes, and rehearsal or performance reminders only.
  • No marketing, promotional, or solicitation content — ever.
  • Frequency: typically 1–2 messages per rehearsal day the user is called to; none otherwise. Quiet hours are enforced (no texts 9pm–7am Pacific).
  • Opt-out: reply STOP to any message, or un-check the box in the app. Reply HELP or email callboard@glowtape.net for help.
  • Phone numbers and consent records are never sold or shared with third parties for their marketing; see the privacy policy.
