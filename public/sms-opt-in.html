How Glow Tape collects SMS opt-in consent

Glow Tape (glowtape.net) is a free scheduling app for community theater volunteers in Grays Harbor County, WA, operated by Zucchini Volcano LLC. SMS is strictly opt-in, and this page documents the exact consent flow inside the app.

The opt-in flow

  1. A user signs in to the app (passwordless, via emailed one-time code).
  2. On their Home screen, an optional “Text reminders” card lets them enter their own cell number.
  3. The number is verified by a one-time code texted to that number — no texts are ever sent to unverified numbers.
  4. Consent is recorded via the checkbox below, which the user may un-check at any time in the app. Replying STOP to any message also opts the user out.

The exact point of consent (as shown in the app)

📱 (360) 555-0123 is verified.

Text me reminders about my calls (message & data rates may apply; reply STOP anytime)

What opted-in users receive

One-time sign-in / verification codes and rehearsal or performance reminders only. No marketing, promotional, or solicitation content is ever sent.
