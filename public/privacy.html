Glow Tape Privacy Policy

Effective July 12, 2026. Glow Tape (glowtape.net) is a free scheduling service for community theaters in Grays Harbor County, WA, operated by Zucchini Volcano LLC.

What we collect

Your name, email address, optional cell phone number, and the theater information you and your productions enter (schedules, conflicts, messages, roles, contact details). That's it — no advertising trackers, no analytics brokers.

How it's used

Solely to operate the service: showing schedules to your production, sending sign-in codes, and sending the email/text reminders you've asked for. Your contact information is visible to members of the productions you join (that's the digital contact sheet) and to no one else.

SMS

Text messages are sent only to users who verify their number and explicitly opt in, and consist only of sign-in codes and rehearsal/performance reminders. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or un-check the box in the app. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Children

Glow Tape accounts are for adults and teens 13 and older. Children under 13 never have accounts or logins; they are represented solely by records their parent or guardian manages (name and role only — no email, phone, or birthdate is collected for any child). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and any such account we discover is deleted. Guardians may review, correct, or delete their child's record at any time: callboard@glowtape.net. See our Youth Safety statement.

Data retention

We keep data only as long as it serves the production or the community, never indefinitely: account and member records are deleted within 90 days of an account-deletion request; production content (schedules, messages, photos) is retained while the production's theater remains active on Glow Tape and deleted within 12 months of a theater leaving the service; sign-in codes expire within 10 minutes and are purged automatically; server backups are retained 14 days locally and up to 90 days offsite, after which deleted data is gone from backups too.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS everywhere) and stored on access-controlled servers; administrative access is limited to the operator; we review access, dependencies, and vendors periodically and keep a written security program.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. Service providers used to deliver the product (email and SMS carriers, server hosting) process data only as needed to deliver messages and host the service.

Your choices

You can leave a production, turn off text reminders, or ask us to delete your account and data entirely at any time: steven@zucchinivolcano.com.
