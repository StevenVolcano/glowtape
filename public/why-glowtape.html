🎭 Glow Tape — the production hub for community theater

Auditions, casting, scheduling, messages, and the paperwork — one place, built for real community theater. glowtape.net

Why it exists

Community theater runs on volunteers, group texts, paper packets, and heroic stage managers. Glow Tape replaces the chaos without asking anyone to be techy: no passwords, no app store, no ads, nothing to install. If someone in your cast can read email, they can use it — and for everyone who can't or won't, everything important also comes out as a clean printout.

How it's designed (the promises)

  • Easy for every age. Big type, big buttons, plain words. Sign in with a 6-digit emailed code — no passwords, ever.
  • Kids are protected. No accounts under 13; parents hold their child's role and get every notice. No private messages anywhere in the app — a "private" note to the production team always includes the whole team. Teens get extra guardrails.
  • Paper still works. Printable audition forms, schedules (list and month-grid), contact sheets, and this page. The tech-averse are first-class citizens.
  • Quiet hours. No notifications between 9pm and 7am. Early calls are announced the evening before, not at dawn.
  • Your data is yours. Export everything as a file anytime. No ads, no data selling, no AI — just a tool.

What it does today

  • Auditions: a public community calendar announces them; a signup form shows your roles as checkboxes, every performance date, and asks each signup to confirm availability for all performances and strike. Custom questions, conflicts, headshots and stage-history résumés arrive attached — no emails flying around. Paper forms print with the same questions.
  • Casting: a private worksheet only the production team sees. Draft from signups, spot double-casting and conflicts at a glance, cast a lead or two early, then finalize — the cast instantly gets the schedule.
  • Scheduling that knows the show: break the show down by song, scene, or pages, mark who's in what, and every "Act II run-through" automatically calls exactly the right people. Conflicts are collected up front and flagged as you schedule.
  • A schedule people actually see: phone calendars sync automatically; changes email everyone affected and reset their "Got it 👍" so you know who's seen the new plan. Printable per-person schedules. QR codes for the callboard.
  • Rehearsal nights: roll call in two taps; running-late/sick reports (parents can send one for their kid) alert the team instantly; every night with a rehearsal, managers get a report — attendance, overdue tasks, the day's notes.
  • Messages & notes: announcements with read-tracking, department channels, app notifications, searchable rehearsal notes you can post into chat.
  • The paperwork: props, costumes, set, and cue trackers (CSV in/out); department to-do lists; program bios requested, written, and compiled in-app.
  • Community: one calendar of every local audition and performance, a message board, and profiles that follow people from show to show.

What it costs

Grays Harbor productions: free, forever. That's the founding promise. Cast, crew, and families never pay anything, anywhere. Theater companies outside Grays Harbor chip in a modest amount that covers what the service costs to run — cost recovery, not profit.
