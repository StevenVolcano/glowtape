Glow Tape Terms of Use

Effective July 12, 2026.

The service

Glow Tape is a free community service for Grays Harbor theater productions, operated by Zucchini Volcano LLC. It is provided as-is, with no warranty and no fee — a volunteer effort, like the theaters it serves.

Your part

Use it for theater production coordination; be decent to your castmates in messages; only upload materials you have the right to share. Directors and stage managers are responsible for the accuracy of their production's schedule.

Youth safety

Productions with child performers must follow the guardian-managed model: children under 13 are added as guardian-managed members, never given accounts. All communication stays in group channels; adults must never use Glow Tape (or any channel) for one-on-one contact with minors. Respect every member's photo-consent flag. See the Youth Safety statement — it is part of these terms.

Content & takedowns

Productions' materials are private to their members. If you believe content on this service infringes your copyright, contact callboard@glowtape.net and it will be addressed promptly.

Termination

Accounts used for spam, harassment, or unlawful content may be removed. You may delete your account at any time.
