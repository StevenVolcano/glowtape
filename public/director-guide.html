🎬 Setting up your show on Glow Tape

From "approved" to "cast has the schedule" — everything happens on your show's Manage tab at glowtape.net unless noted.

  1. Get your show Home screen → Request a production. When it's approved you get an email with your join code and the Manage tab appears on your show.
  2. Set your places Manage → Event types & places: save your usual rehearsal spots with street addresses — locations then autofill when scheduling, show as map links, and land in people's phone calendars. Adjust the event-type list if you like (Auditions, Table Read, Blocking… are built in).
  3. Break down the show Sheets tab → Breakdown: choose by-song (musical), by-scene, or by-pages, then mark who's in each unit — or import a spreadsheet (CSV with columns like Song, Act, Pages, On stage, Singing, Dancing; people are matched by role name). This is what lets scheduling call exactly the right people.
  4. Add the roles Manage → People & roles: every character and crew position, before anyone is cast. Each role gets a claim code. Check "This is a child" for young performers — a parent claims that code, never the child.
  5. Open auditions Manage → Auditions: check the box, add dates/place/what-to-prepare and your own questions (one per line). Tap 📤 Share the audition link and post it — signups collect right on your Manage tab with each person's profile and conflicts. Print blank paper forms for the audition table. Newcomers to Glow Tape also need a signup code: your join code works, or a community code from the organizer.
  6. Put audition nights on the schedule Manage → Add to the schedule, type Auditions — so the time, place, and map link are one tap away.
  7. Cast night Hand each person their role's claim code (or 📤 the role's invite link). When they join with it, their entire schedule is already waiting. Uncheck Auditions open when you're done.
  8. Build the rehearsal schedule Repeats weekly creates the whole run in one pass and sends the cast one summary email. Pick breakdown units on each event and the right people are called automatically. The Fix up the schedule table adjusts many nights at once — also one email.
  9. Read-through night Print the getting-started handout, write the join code in the blank, hand it out. Then nudge everyone: enter your conflicts now — the Manage tab shows you every conflict that collides with a call, as a to-do.
Along the way: post rehearsal tracks and forms under Documents & links · take rehearsal notes on the Notes tab and post them to a channel · request program bios when publicity asks · roll call and late/sick alerts run themselves from the Schedule tab.

Stuck? glowtape.net/help.html or email callboard@glowtape.net — a human reads it.
