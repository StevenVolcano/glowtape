📱 Get your rehearsal schedule on your phone

Glow Tape — free for Grays Harbor theater. No app store, no password, no cost.

glowtape.net
  1. Open glowtape.net in your phone's web browser.
  2. Type your name and email, tap Email me a sign-in code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code from your email. (Not there? Check spam — it's from callboard@glowtape.net. Add that address to your contacts!)
  4. Under Join a production, enter this code:   
  5. You're in! Tap Got it 👍 on rehearsals so your stage manager knows you saw them, and enter your conflicts (days you're NOT available) right away.

Want it like an app? (optional)

iPhone: in Safari tap Share (□↑) → Add to Home Screen.
Android: in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Want text reminders? (optional)

On your Glow Tape home screen, find Text reminders, add your cell number, and type the code we text you. You'll get a reminder before every call.

Questions? See glowtape.net/help.html or ask your stage manager.
