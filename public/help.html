Glow Tape Help

The production hub for Grays Harbor theater — schedule, messages, to-dos, notes, and the paperwork, all in one place. No passwords, no downloads required, no cost — ever.

Jump to: Getting started · Cast & crew · Directors & stage managers · Kids & families · Feedback

Getting started

How do I sign in?

Go to glowtape.net, enter your name, email, and your code, and tap Email me a sign-in code. A 6-digit code arrives by email — type it in and you're in. There is no password to remember, ever. (Once you've added and verified a cell number, you can choose Text me a code instead.)

First time? Creating an account needs a code: the join code from your production, a role code from your director, or a community code from the organizer. If you used a production or role code, you land straight in your show. Signing back in later needs no code.

The code email didn't come.

Check your spam or junk folder — the sender is callboard@glowtape.net. Adding that address to your contacts fixes it permanently. Codes expire after a few minutes, so if it's been a while, just request a fresh one.

How do I join my show?

Your director or stage manager will give you a 6-letter join code (at the read-through, or by text/email). On your Glow Tape home screen, type it under Join a production and tap Join. That's it — you'll see the show's schedule immediately.

Can I put Glow Tape on my home screen like an app?

iPhone: open glowtape.net in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.
Android: open glowtape.net in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
It works fine in a regular browser too — this is optional.

What are all the tabs?

Inside a production: Schedule — every rehearsal and performance, your conflicts, calendar links. Messages — announcements and group channels. To-Do — task lists by department, plus your program-bio box when one's been requested. Sheets — the show breakdown (your songs/scenes) plus props, costumes, set, and cue trackers. Notes — rehearsal notes anyone can read. People — who's who in the show (contact details are production-team only). The production team also sees Casting and Manage.

For cast & crew

What does "Got it 👍" do?

It tells your stage manager you've seen that rehearsal or change. Tap it on every event you're called to — it saves them from chasing people down. If an event's time or place changes, you'll get an email and your "Got it" resets so you can confirm you saw the new plan.

What are conflicts?

Days you're not available — work trips, weddings, anything. Enter them under My conflicts on the Schedule tab as early as possible, so the schedule gets built around them instead of colliding with them.

How do I get notifications on my phone?

On your home screen, find Notifications and tap 🔔 Turn on notifications — messages, schedule changes, and task pings then arrive like any other app's notifications. It's per-device, so turn it on on both your phone and computer if you like. iPhone: add Glow Tape to your Home Screen first (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there — Apple only allows notifications for installed apps. Muted channels never ping you.

How do I get text reminders?

On your home screen, find Text reminders, enter your cell number, and type the code we text you. You'll get a text about 10 hours before each call and again about 2 hours out. Texts never arrive between 9pm and 7am — an early-morning call is announced the evening before instead. Reply STOP to any text (or un-check the box) to turn them off. Email reminders happen regardless.

Can I print my schedule?

Yes — on the Schedule tab, tap 🖨 Print my schedule for a paper-friendly page with a chronological list and monthly calendar grids of just your calls. Behind on "Got it"s? 👍 Got them all acknowledges every upcoming event at once (it asks first — only use it if you've really read them).

What's the community calendar?

Home → Theater Community: every audition and performance across all Grays Harbor productions on one calendar, plus the community message board. Rehearsals and everything else stay private to each show.

Can I see rehearsals on my own calendar?

Yes, two ways. On any event, tap + Google Calendar or + Apple / other calendar to add that one. Or, at the bottom of the Schedule tab, tap Get my calendar link and subscribe once — every event you're called to appears in your calendar and stays up to date. (Google can take a few hours to refresh subscribed calendars; the app and texts are always current.)

What's my profile for?

Your community profile (Home → My profile) is your acting résumé: a stage-history table (year, company, show, role), skills, an optional headshot. It travels with you from show to show, anyone signed in can see it, and directors see it next to your audition signups. All of it is optional. (Members under 18 don't upload headshots — directors take photos at auditions if needed.)

How do auditions work?

When a director opens auditions, the show appears under Auditions on everyone's home screen and on the community calendar (with a signup link). The form shows the audition dates and places, the roles on offer as checkboxes (check everything that appeals — it's interest, not a commitment), your conflicts, and the director's questions. You can come back and update your answers any time until auditions close.

Where are the rehearsal tracks / scripts / forms?

At the bottom of your show's Schedule tab, under Resources — the production team posts documents and links there (rehearsal tracks, character breakdowns, forms). Audition materials appear right on the audition signup form.

Can I message the production team privately?

Yes. In your show's Messages tab, tap 🔒 Message the production team privately. That opens a channel between you and the whole production team — director, stage manager, and the rest — for anything you'd rather not post to the full cast (a costume worry, a scheduling crunch, a question). It's semi-private on purpose: there are never one-on-one messages on Glow Tape, and parents of young members see their child's team channel too.

Running late or sick on rehearsal day?

Open the event on your Schedule tab (available from 24 hours before) and tap 🕒 Running late or 😷 Can't make it, add a note if you like, and your production team is alerted immediately by email (and text, where enabled). No hunting for the stage manager's number. Parents see the same buttons for each child they manage — the alert tells the team it's about your child, reported by you.

Why do names in chat look like "Anna K. (Golde)"?

Inside a show, everyone's messages are signed with their first name, last initial, and their role or character — so you always know whether that's Anna from the ensemble or Anna the stage manager. On the community board it's just the name.

How do I mute a chatty channel?

Open the channel in Messages and tap the 🔔 line to mute it. You can still read it any time — muting only affects notifications.

For directors & stage managers

Getting your show onto Glow Tape

On your home screen, tap Request a production and answer a few questions — theater, title, timeline, whether kids are involved. The organizer reviews it (usually quickly) and you'll get an email with your join code and the Manage tab ready to go.

Setting up a new show, step by step

Your To-Do tab opens with a setup checklist (production team only) that walks you from approval to opening auditions to the full rehearsal schedule — and checks itself off as you go. Prefer paper? Print the one-page Director's Setup Guide for the production binder.

Where's my join code?

Manage tab → Invite people. Share the big dashed code at the read-through, print the read-through handout and write it in, or tap 📱 Show QR code and let people scan it off your phone (or a printout) — scanning lands them on signup with the code already filled in. Auditions have their own QR under Manage → Auditions.

Can I invite someone with a link?

Yes — in Manage, tap 📤 Share invite link (or 📤 next to any role) and text or email it. Opening the link fills the code in automatically: new folks sign up and land straight in the show; signed-in folks just join.

Which events show up on the community calendar?

Anything with an Auditions or Performance event type is listed publicly — title, time, and place only, never who's called or your notes. The event form shows a 🌍 note whenever you're creating one, so it's never a surprise.

Scheduling a whole rehearsal run at once

In Manage → Add to the schedule, check Repeats weekly, tap the weekdays, set the end date. Up to 60 events are created and the cast gets one summary email.

Fixing up several events at once

Manage → Fix up the schedule is a table of upcoming events — change types, titles, days, times, places in bulk, then Save all. Everyone called gets one email covering everything that actually moved; fixing typos bothers nobody.

Event types and preset places

Manage → Event types & places. Customize the event-type list (Table Read, Blocking, Dance…) and save your usual rehearsal places with street addresses — locations then autofill when scheduling, show up as tappable map links, and ride along into people's calendars. Places saved on your theater's organization are shared across its productions.

Announcements

Manage → Post an announcement for the things everyone must see — it's pinned atop the Messages tab and emailed to the whole production, with a "Got it" so you can see who's read it.

Seeing the schedule through one person's eyes

On the Schedule tab, pick a name under View as… to see exactly what that cast member sees — handy for "wait, why does Emma think she's off Thursday?" — and print their personal schedule (list + monthly calendars) from the same spot.

The Casting tab

Your private casting worksheet — invisible to everyone outside the production team. Draft the cast with dropdowns fed by audition signups, people already in the show (🎭, at the bottom of each list), or ✍ someone from a paper form — type their name and it's recorded on the role as a not-on-Glow-Tape member. Double-cast picks get a ⚠ flag and each pick's conflicts show inline, so you can see problems while you think (nothing has to be resolved to keep drafting). When you're sure, Finalize the cast — after a you-are-sure warning — and everyone drafted is attached to their role and sees the show. Child roles are still claimed by parents with their role codes.

Cast members who won't use the app

In Manage → People & roles, tap 📇 Not on Glow Tape? on their role and record their name, email, and phone. They'll get the production's emails, appear on your contact sheet, and never get texts (they didn't opt in).

Taking roll (stage managers)

On any event, tap Roll call and tap names to cycle present → late → absent. Self-reports from cast appear there automatically, and the event card shows running totals. Manage → Attendance history keeps the season-long tally per person (production team only), and on any day with something scheduled, the team gets a nightly rehearsal report email at 10pm — roll call, who was late or out, overdue to-dos, and the notes written that day.

What happens when I edit or cancel an event?

Changing the date, time, or place automatically emails everyone called and resets their "Got it" so you can see who's seen the change. Cancelling emails everyone and crosses it out (it can be restored). Fixing a typo in the title or notes doesn't bother anyone.

Can I build the schedule before auditions?

Yes. In Manage → People & roles, add the roles themselves (Ophelia, Ensemble, Light Board Op) before anyone is cast, and call those roles on events as you build the schedule. Each role gets a claim code (the join code plus two letters, like TR7PDQ-XY). On cast night, hand each person their role's code — when they join with it, their entire schedule is already waiting for them.

The To-Do tab

Simple task lists by department — set build, props, costumes, publicity. Managers add tasks with an optional assignee and due date (assignees get one email); whoever it's assigned to checks it off. Overdue items turn red. That's the whole system, on purpose. (When bios are requested, each person's bio box appears at the top of their To-Do tab.)

Holding auditions

Manage → Auditions: check the box to open them, add the details (dates, place, what to prepare) and any questions of your own, one per line. Every Glow Tape user sees the show on their home screen and can sign up; signups collect right there in Manage — each one shows their answers, conflicts, and community profile (headshot, experience, skills). No emails fly around; check the list whenever you like. Uncheck the box to close auditions. Name your performer roles in People & roles first and they appear on the signup form as checkboxes (and on the paper form as check-off boxes), so you learn who wants what. Audition-type events from your schedule show on the form automatically. For the paper crowd, Print blank paper forms gives you a hand-fillable version with the same questions, and sides or packets can be attached under Documents & links (area: Auditions) so every auditioner can open them.

Sharing documents and links

Manage → Documents & links. Give it a title, then paste a link or attach a file (PDFs, tracks — up to 25 MB), and choose whether it's for the show (cast and crew see it at the bottom of the Schedule tab) or for auditions (it appears on the signup form).

The Notes tab (rehearsal notes)

After a run-through, tap + New note — the title is pre-filled with the date — and type your notes. Then pick a channel and tap 💬 Post to chat: the note appears in the channel as a tappable link showing its title. (🔗 Copy link is there too if you'd rather paste it yourself.) Anyone in the show can write notes; you (and the production team) can edit yours later, and everyone can read them all on the Notes tab. The search box above the list looks inside every note — type "fainting couch" and see exactly which nights it came up.

The show breakdown (Sheets → Breakdown)

Break the show into units and scheduling gets smart. If you change who's in a unit after events are scheduled, tap ↻ Re-sync the schedule on the Breakdown to update the call lists on upcoming events (quietly — nobody is emailed). Pick the shape that fits: by song for a musical (each song tracks who's on stage, who sings, who dances), by scene, or by pages/hunks for a straight play. Build it by hand — tap a unit, toggle the roles involved — or import a CSV with columns like Song, Act, Pages, On stage, Singing, Dancing, Notes; people are matched by their role or character name from People & roles, so it works before anyone is cast. Then, when adding to the schedule, pick the units you're rehearsing and everyone involved is called automatically. Cast members can tap Just mine to see exactly which songs or scenes are theirs.

The Sheets tab (props, costumes, set, cues)

This replaces the multi-tab production Google Sheet. Pick a sheet — Props, Costumes, Set, Light cues, Sound cues — and the production team edits right in the table: tap a cell, type, tap away, it's saved. Everyone in the show can read them; only managers can edit. Moving off an old spreadsheet? In Google Sheets do File → Download → Comma Separated Values (one tab at a time), then tap ⬆ Import CSV on the matching sheet — columns are matched by their headers. ⬇ Export CSV gets your data back out any time, and 🖨 Print makes paper copies for the booth.

Collecting program bios

Manage → Program bios → Request bios from the cast. Everyone missing a bio gets a to-do (and an email; guardians get their child's). People write theirs right on the To-Do tab, it checks off their task automatically, and the compiled page gives you the whole program section to print or copy in one tap.

Who gets the Manage tab?

Anyone with the Manage box checked in Manage → People & roles. It checks itself automatically for directors, assistant directors, and stage managers (including when they claim a staff role code), and you can grant it to anyone else — producer, choreographer, music director. Managers see the Manage tab, everyone's conflicts, acknowledgment counts, and roll call.

🛡️ Kids & families

How do children join a show?

They don't sign up — a parent or guardian does. The director adds the child as a role in Manage (checking "This is a child"), which creates a code. A parent claims that code (or its invite link) with their own account, and from then on the parent sees the child's full schedule, gets every reminder, taps "Got it," and reads every group message the child's cast can see. Children under 13 never have logins — that's by design, not a limitation. Read more: Youth Safety at Glow Tape.

Both parents want the schedule.

Share the same child code with both — every guardian who claims it independently gets everything. Grandparents driving to rehearsal too, if you like.

Photos of kids

Every member has a photo-consent setting (Manage → People & roles → 📷). If anyone in a production has photos turned off, the app reminds everyone before pictures get posted. When in doubt, don't post faces.

How do I report a worrying message?

Tap the + under any message, then the ⚑ flag. It goes straight to the Glow Tape operator — a real person, and deliberately not the production's own managers — who will look at it promptly. Use it for anything that feels off; you won't get anyone in trouble by asking a human to take a look.

Why are there no private messages?

On purpose, permanently. Group-only conversation means no adult can privately message a minor through Glow Tape — the same open-communication standard used by youth sports and scouting nationwide.

Ideas, problems, wishes

On your home screen, find Ideas & feedback, pick a flavor (idea, something broken, question, something nice), and send it. Every submission is read by a human, and you'll see its status — received, planned, done — right under the form on later visits.

Still stuck?

Email callboard@glowtape.net — a human reads it.
